# PRODUCT.md

## Milestone 1 Product Definition

Cal Menut is a lightweight operational portal for centralising day-to-day knowledge about the property, its people, and its supporting records.

## Goals

- Provide a clear entry point for the five approved domains: Masia, Infraestructures, Equips, Gestions, and Arxiu.
- Keep Milestone 1 intentionally simple with navigable placeholder pages.
- Establish product language and structure that can scale into richer workflows later.

## Non-goals

- Full authentication and permissions.
- Production data import.
- Complex workflow automation.

## Users

- Property coordinators who need a shared operational overview.
- Team members who need a reliable place to find current context.
- Maintainers who will extend each domain after Milestone 1.
